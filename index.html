Most SEO fails because it's done piecemeal. We sequence technical, content, and authority work so each one compounds the last.

01 / Technical

Technical & on-site SEO

Crawl architecture, Core Web Vitals, structured data, and indexation cleanup — so search engines can actually find and trust what you've built.

How an engagement actually runs.

The same four stages, every time — because consistency is what makes the results predictable.

01
Week 1–2

Audit & baseline

Full technical crawl, backlink profile review, and competitor gap analysis. You get a scored baseline before we touch anything.

02
Week 3–4

Roadmap & priorities

Findings get ranked by traffic impact vs. effort. You approve the roadmap — no work starts on anything you haven't signed off on.

03
Month 2 onward

Build & publish

Technical fixes ship, content goes into production on a fixed cadence, and outreach for links begins in parallel.

04
Ongoing

Measure & compound

Monthly reporting tied to revenue, not vanity metrics. Winning pages get doubled down on; underperformers get revised or cut.
